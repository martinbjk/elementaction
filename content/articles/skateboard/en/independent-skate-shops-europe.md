## Why this list exists

Skateboarding has more riders than ever, but fewer people who can make a living from it. Brands have been bought and sold with borrowed money, and in February 2025 the operator behind Quiksilver, Billabong and Volcom went bankrupt with more than $220 million in debt. Meanwhile kids buy decks online, often from the same brands that local shops stock.

The independent shop is where that money used to turn into something real: a team, a video, a jam, a free board for the kid who can't afford one. Every shop below does at least some of that.

**🔎 Looking for a shop near you?** Use the [Skate Shop Finder](/en/tools/skate-shop-finder/) to search every shop on this list by city, country or name, with a map link for each one.

**What counts as "independent" here:** a physical store, run by skaters or with skateboarding at its core, and not part of a big retail chain. Independent surf shops with a real skateboard section count too, since along the coast they often are the local skate shop. Chains like Blue Tomato, Junkyard, Zumiez, doodah and Decathlon, and pure web shops, are left out on purpose.

**How each shop was checked:** an entry is marked ✅ **Verified** only when the street address comes from the shop's own website, from an official city or tourism listing, or from at least two independent sources that agree. Shops I could only partly confirm are listed in the "Still to be verified" table at the bottom. Shops move and close, so if you spot an error, let us know.

## Nordics

### Sweden

#### Streetlab — Malmö ✅
- **Address:** Regementsgatan 12, 211 42 Malmö
- **Web:** streetlab.nu
- **Since:** 2001
- Malmö's best-known core shop: hardgoods, shoes and its own Streetlab line. It has released full shop videos, *Lemonade* (2020) and *LUCCA* (2025), featuring the city's locals.

#### Bryggeriet Skateshop — Malmö ✅
- **Address:** Ystadvägen 44, 214 45 Malmö (inside Bryggeriet indoor skatepark)
- **Web:** skatemalmo.se
- A fully stocked shop inside the 2,000 m² indoor park that kick-started Malmö's skate scene in 1998.

#### Stigberget Skateshop — Gothenburg ✅
- **Address:** Stigbergsliden 1, 414 63 Göteborg (between Majorna and Linnéstaden)
- **Web:** stigbergetskateshop.se
- Hardware and clothing from selected brands, plus the shop's own collection designed by Gorm Boberg. Get off the tram at Masthuggstorget or Stigbergstorget.

#### Standtall — Gothenburg ✅
- **Address:** Aröds Industriväg 40, Göteborg
- **Web:** standtall.se
- **Store since:** 2020
- A skate and snowboard shop that runs a physical store in the same building as its warehouse, so everything on the website is in the store. Free parking outside.

#### EVA Hardware — Stockholm ✅
- **Address:** Bondegatan 48, Stockholm (Södermalm)
- **Web:** evahardware.com
- **Note:** Open Wednesday to Saturday only, so check the website for hours.
- A skateboard and vintage shop run by the EVA Skateboards crew. It made a first-of-its-kind metal riser pad for Ville Wester.

#### Street Style — Stockholm ✅
- **Address:** Hamngatan 10 (PK-huset), 111 47 Stockholm
- **Web:** streetstyle08.com
- **Since:** 1988
- A skateboard and streetwear store in the PK-huset arcade in central Stockholm, with decks, hardware, clothing and a second-hand section. Its roots go back to the Stockholm skate scene of the late 1980s.

#### FuckIt Skateboards — Norrköping ✅
- **Address:** Tenngatan 13, 602 23 Norrköping (inside the Ladan skatehall)
- **Web:** fuckitskateboards.com
- A small local board brand that prints its own graphics in Norrköping. You can pick graphic, shape and width for a custom board. The boards are sold in the Ladan indoor skatehall and online.

### Norway

#### SHIT® Skate Shop — Stavanger ✅
- **Address:** Øvre Holmegate 11, 2nd floor, 4006 Stavanger
- **Web:** shitskateboardcompany.com
- **Note:** Limited opening hours, so check the website before you go.
- The shop of SHIT®, Norway's longest-running skateboard brand. It has been skater-owned since day one and sits up a narrow staircase off Stavanger's colourful "Fargegata" street.

#### Lille Løkka — Oslo ✅
- **Address:** Torggata 22, 0183 Oslo
- **Web:** lillelokka.com
- A local Oslo skateboard and streetwear shop in the city centre.

### Denmark

#### Sidewalk Skateshop — Copenhagen ✅
- **Address:** Enghave Plads 10, 1670 København V
- **Web:** sidewalkshop.dk
- **Since:** 2003
- An original Vesterbro shop, a short push from Copenhagen Skatepark and the Enghave Plads spot.

#### Circus Circus — Copenhagen ✅
- **Address:** Guldbergsgade 16 (basement, right), 2200 København N (Nørrebro)
- **Web:** circuscircus.dk
- **Since:** 2008
- A 100% skateboard shop with a circus theme, full of curiosities, figures and stickers.

#### do you! — Copenhagen ✅
- **Address:** Blågårdsgade 7A, 2200 København N (Nørrebro)
- **Web:** doyou-shop.dk
- It started from a living room and a pop-up and is now a "skateshop & more" concept store on Blågårdsgade.

#### Pasteelo — Copenhagen ✅
- **Address:** Frederiksborggade 31, 1360 København K (near Nørreport station)
- **Web:** pasteelo.com
- A skate shop, music store and clothing brand in one.

### Finland

#### My Favorite Things — Helsinki ✅
- **Address:** Fredrikinkatu 31 (courtyard), 00120 Helsinki
- **Web:** myfavoritethings.fi
- **Since:** 2014
- 100% skater-owned. All three owners have skated since the late 1990s. Take tram 3 to Iso-Roobertinkatu.

#### Beyond — Helsinki ✅
- **Address:** Kaisaniemenkatu 4, 00100 Helsinki
- **Since:** 2006
- A Finnish street fashion and skateboarding store in the city centre.

#### Ponke's The Shop — Helsinki ✅
- **Address:** Simonkatu 6 (Forum shopping centre), 00100 Helsinki
- **Since:** 1990
- One of Helsinki's oldest skate and snowboard shops.

#### Shreddington Snow + Lamina — Helsinki ✅
- **Address:** Kalevankatu 31, 00100 Helsinki
- **Web:** shreddingtonsnow.com
- A snowboard specialist that took over the Lamina skate store and now carries skate and surf gear as well.

## Rest of Europe

### United Kingdom

#### Slam City Skates — London ✅
- **Address:** The Old Truman Brewery, 10 Dray Walk, London E1 6QL (off Brick Lane)
- **Web:** slamcity.com
- A London institution that started in Covent Garden and now has its main store in the East End. All the staff skate.

### France

#### Nozbone Skateboard Boutique — Paris ✅
- **Address:** 7 Rue de Marseille, 75010 Paris
- **Web:** nozbone.com
- **Since:** 2003
- Between République and Canal Saint-Martin. It was named European Skateshop of the Year in 2016 and is known for its Benchmark contest and for a concrete mini ramp hidden in the cellar.

#### VEGA Skateshop — Paris ✅
- **Address:** 18 Rue Louis Blanc, 75010 Paris
- A short push from Nozbone, which makes the 10th arrondissement a two-shop stop.

### Spain

#### Welcome Skateboarding — Madrid ✅
- **Address:** Calle Corredera Alta de San Pablo 16, 28004 Madrid (Malasaña)
- **Web:** welcomesk8.com
- **Note:** Open Monday to Saturday, 12:00–20:00.

#### Sk8land — Madrid ✅
- **Address:** Calle de Valverde 19, 28004 Madrid
- **Web:** sk8land.com
- **Since:** 2002
- A "100% skateboard shop" for shoes, hardware and clothing.

#### Dagger Family — Madrid ✅
- **Address:** Calle de Ceuta 1, 28039 Madrid
- Skate shop, tattoo and piercing studio, barbershop, indoor ramp and skate school under one roof.

### Germany

#### Civilist — Berlin ✅
- **Address:** Brunnenstraße 13, 10119 Berlin (Mitte)
- **Web:** civilistberlin.com
- **Since:** 2009
- Part skate shop, part streetwear label, part gallery, in a former art space. It is independently run and has its own brand, stocked worldwide.

#### Bonkers — Frankfurt am Main ✅
- **Address:** Klappergasse 11, 60594 Frankfurt (Sachsenhausen)
- **Web:** bonkers-shop.com
- **Since:** 2010
- It started online, selling European skater-owned brands, and now runs a physical shop and its own private indoor park, the "Bonkers Vereinsheim".

#### Railslide — Frankfurt am Main ✅
- **Address:** Neue Kräme 34, 60311 Frankfurt
- One of the oldest shops in the Rhine-Main area, trading for more than 30 years.

### Netherlands

#### Ben-G — Amsterdam ✅
- **Address:** Nieuwezijds Voorburgwal 142, 1012 SH Amsterdam
- **Since:** 2005
- Skater-owned by Benny Komala, who still skates every week. The shop spans three floors and is known worldwide for its Nike SB, Converse and Patta collaborations.

### Belgium

#### Lockwood — Antwerp ✅
- **Addresses:** Lockwood Hardgoods, Lange Klarenstraat 29 · Lockwood Avenue (skate + sneakers), IJzerenwaag 1, 2000 Antwerpen
- **Web:** lockwood-avenue.com
- **Since:** 1996
- Founded by Belgian pro Sven Aerts, who chose to open a shop instead of chasing a pro career. It was named best European skate shop in 2014. The Hardgoods store has a deck wall of 100 boards above a skate bowl.

### Switzerland

#### Rollladen — Zürich ✅
- **Address:** Konradstrasse 72, 8005 Zürich
- **Web:** roll-laden.tv
- Skateboards and longboards, with a test-board centre for trying before you buy.

### Austria

#### Freedom Skateshop — Vienna ✅
- **Address:** Zieglergasse 28, 1070 Wien (Neubau)
- **Web:** freedomskateshop.at
- Known for its huge deck wall with hundreds of boards.

### Czech Republic

#### StreetMarket — Prague ✅
- **Address:** Husitská 13, Praha 3 (Žižkov)
- **Web:** streetmarket.cz
- Decks, hardware, shoes and clothing from core skate brands.

#### Popcorn — Prague ✅ *(Boardstar group)*
- **Address:** Milady Horákové 397/32, Praha 7 (Letná, tram stop Kamenická)
- **Web:** popcornshop.cz
- Nearly 500 m², one of the largest board-sports stores in Central Europe, with its own skate room.

#### El Niño — Prague ✅ *(Boardstar group)*
- **Address:** Palackého náměstí 2, Praha 2
- **Web:** elninoshop.cz
- A three-floor skate and snow shop near the riverside, trading for more than 20 years.

#### Easy Rider — Prague ✅ *(Boardstar group)*
- **Address:** Jugoslávská 15/180, Praha 2
- **Web:** easyridershop.cz
- One of Prague's first snow and skateboard shops.

#### Revolution — Prague ✅ *(Boardstar group)*
- **Address:** Revoluční 2, Praha 1 (opposite Kotva department store)
- **Web:** revolutionshop.cz

### Poland

#### California Skateshop — Warsaw ✅
- **Address:** Aleja Armii Ludowej 12, 00-638 Warszawa
- **Web:** californiaskateshop.pl
- Calls itself the biggest and oldest skate shop in Warsaw and stocks Polish classics like Nervous, Fish and Prosto alongside international brands. It has a sister store in Łódź.

#### SH Store — Warsaw ✅
- **Address:** Oleandrów 3, 00-629 Warszawa
- **Web:** shstore.eu
- A core skateboard shop, online and in store.

### Portugal

#### Cybercafé® — Lisbon ✅
- **Address:** Rua do Ferragial 1, 1200 Lisboa (between Baixa-Chiado and Cais do Sodré)
- **Web:** cybercafeskateshop.com
- **Since:** 2021
- Skater-owned and run as both a shop and a cultural hub, with video premieres, magazine launches and Go Skateboarding Day. Its full-length video is *1500 Dias*.

### Italy

#### 7Hills — Rome ✅
- **Address:** Via Flaminia 47, 00196 Roma (near Metro A Flaminio)
- **Web:** 7hillsroma.com
- **Since:** 2012
- Rome's core shop, with a pro team, tours, contests and its own distribution. Closed Sundays ("time to skate").
