## Intro

At the Los Angeles Olympics, skateboarding will be held from **18 to 27 July 2028**, with park and street for men and women ([NBC Olympics](https://www.nbcolympics.com/news/skateboarding-2028-los-angeles-olympics)). It is the sport's first Games as a permanent part of the Olympic programme, and it takes place in Southern California, where skateboarding has deep roots.

This article looks at how the main skateboarding nations are preparing: how much money they have, how they are organised, how many skaters are in their national programmes, and how they help young skaters through qualification. Sources are linked directly in the text. Where we could not find a public figure, we say so.

For the bigger picture of the industry and the culture, see our article [The State of Skateboarding 2026](/en/skateboard/articles/state-of-skateboarding-2026/). For surfing, see [Road to LA28: the surfing nations](/en/surfing/articles/la28-olympic-surfing-how-nations-prepare/).

## In short

- **22 skaters per event**, a maximum of **three per country** in each event. Qualification runs from June 2026 to June 2028.
- **Skaters must be born in 2014 or earlier** to compete at LA28.
- **Japan** pays 100% of international travel for its top three skaters in each event, and has a national training base for skateboarding. Japanese skaters took 7 of 12 podium places at the first qualifier.
- **Brazil** gets about **R$11.3 million** from the national lottery in 2026 and has a national team of **34 skaters**.
- **Great Britain** raised its skateboarding budget to **£2.55 million** for 2025–2029.
- **France** says openly that its target is gold, and that "participation cannot be an objective".
- **The USA** has filled all 12 places at both previous Games, but has never won Olympic skateboarding gold.

## How the numbers should be read

We mark the most important figures as an *official figure* (published by a government, Olympic committee or federation) or *reported* (from journalists, not audited). Budgets are hard to compare: some cover four years and some one, some cover only skateboarding and some a whole roller-sports federation.

## How to qualify for LA28

World Skate, the international federation, runs qualification in two phases ([World Skate](https://www.skateboarding.worldskate.org/news/1819-la28-qualification-schedule-release.html)):

| Phase | Dates | What counts |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1** | 11 June 2026 – 31 March 2028 | World Skateboarding Tour events. The World Skateboarding Ranking uses the best results from the previous 18 months. |
| **Phase 2** | 1 April – 11 June 2028 | A separate contest series for the **top 44** in each event, with a maximum of six per country. |
| **Final ranking** | 11 June 2028 | Decides the places. |

- **Places:** [22 skaters per event](https://www.surfertoday.com/skateboarding/la-2028-olympic-skateboarding-qualification-system), with a maximum of **three per country** in each event. One universality place per event and gender goes to a country with few Olympic athletes.
- **Olympic Qualifier Series:** Skateboarding is part of three of the IOC's four Q-Series stops: **Tokyo (4–7 May 2028)** with park and street, and **Montreal (1–4 June)** and **Orlando (8–11 June)** with park ([World Skate](https://www.worldskate.org/news/44-discipline/skateboarding/3909-la28-q-series-update.html)). World Skate will add more Phase 2 events. For Japan, the first decisive contest is a home event.
- **Age limit:** Skaters must be [born in 2014 or earlier](https://www.nbcsports.com/olympics/news/skateboarding-age-minimum-olympics-2028-los-angeles), so the youngest possible Olympian in LA will be 13 or 14.
- **The first qualifier** was the World Cup in Rome in June 2026, where Japanese skaters took 7 of the 12 podium places ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/michellebruton/2026/06/26/la28-skateboard-park-street-standings-after-first-olympic-qualifier/)).

## Country by country

### Japan: a clear, paid ladder

Japan's federation, World Skate Japan, publishes exactly what its national team gets ([2026 selection criteria](https://worldskatejapan.or.jp/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2026年度WSJ強化指定選手選考基準と処遇.pdf), *official figure*):

- **Team size:** The **top 8 in the national ranking** in each event and gender become designated national team skaters. With four events, that is up to **32 skaters**.
- **Level A (ranked 1–3):** **100% of international competition travel** and 100% of national training camps paid, and access to the national training centre's skateboarding base in **Murakami, Niigata**.
- **Level B (ranked 4–8):** **75%** of international travel paid.
- **Talent:** Skaters who are too young for World Skate's age limit but good enough for the team are placed in a separate **special development** category instead.
- **Sponsors:** The national championships and Japan Open are backed by Nike SB, the confectionery maker Bourbon and the skate chain Murasaki Sports ([Japan Open notice](https://worldskatejapan.or.jp/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/4th_NihonOpen_youkou.pdf)).
- **Local hubs:** World Skate has signed a [six-year agreement](https://theherdwick.com/2025/02/13/world-skate-signs-six-year-deal-with-japan-after-success-of-2024-world-skate-games-italia/) with the town of Sakai in Ibaraki, less than 100 km from Tokyo, to bring World Cup and World Skateboarding Tour events there and build training facilities and academies.
- **Medal bonuses:** The Japanese Olympic Committee pays **¥5 million for gold, ¥2 million for silver and ¥1 million for bronze**, tax-free ([Financial Field](https://financial-field.com/living/entry-228299)).
- **Money:** The state-funded "competitiveness programme" for all Olympic and Paralympic sports had a budget of about **¥10.4 billion** in 2025 ([Sports Agency](https://www.mext.go.jp/sports/content/20250528-spt_kyosport-300000863_1.pdf)). We have not found the share that goes to skateboarding: the Japan Sport Council publishes the amount for each federation every year ([JSC press releases](https://www.jpnsport.go.jp/sinko/tabid/1844/Default.aspx)), but we could not open the latest list.

### Brazil: a big squad and lottery money

- **Money:** Brazil's Olympic committee (COB) passes lottery money to the federations. Skateboarding gets **R$11.338 million** in 2026 (*official figure*, [Máquina do Esporte](https://maquinadoesporte.com.br/los-angeles-2028/cob-aprova-orcamento-de-r-6786-milhoes-para-2026-e-repassa-42-as-confederacoes/)). All 12 skaters who went to Paris also received the government's monthly [Bolsa Atleta](https://maquinadoesporte.com.br/olimpiadas-paris-2024/balanco-paris-2024-skate-leva-duas-medalhas-e-espera-mais-tranquilidade-em-primeiro-ciclo-completo/) athlete grant.
- **Team size:** The national federation, CBSk, has a national team of **34 skaters** in park and street, after adding eight park skaters from a new trial in Curitiba, including 17-year-old **Gui Khury** ([Lance](https://www.lance.com.br/mais-esportes/confederacao-de-skate-convoca-mais-atletas-para-selecao-de-park.html)).
- **Organisation:** In January 2024 World Skate disaffiliated CBSk and wanted the roller-sports federation to run skateboarding in Brazil. Skaters protested, and the Court of Arbitration for Sport [returned control to CBSk](https://www.opovo.com.br/esportes/2024/02/27/corte-arbitral-do-esporte-devolve-gerencia-do-skate-brasileiro-a-cbsk.html) in a provisional ruling a month later. CBSk now runs the team for the LA28 cycle, which the federation expects to be "calmer" as the sport's first full four-year cycle.
- **Industry:** The vice-president of CBSk [says](https://timesbrasil.com.br/esportes/skate-atrai-marcas-de-fora-da-modalidade-com-avanco-entre-jovens-diz-vice-presidente-da-cbsk/) skate brands have struggled while brands from outside skateboarding have moved in as sponsors.

### USA: depth, but no gold

- **Team size:** The US filled all **12 places** in both Tokyo and Paris ([NBC Olympics](https://www.nbcolympics.com/news/skateboarding-101-us-olympic-roster-and-athlete-news?chrcontext=ktvz)). USA Skateboarding names a national team each year. In 2023 it had [16 skaters](https://girlisnota4letterword.com/blog/usa-skateboarding-announces-2023-team-riders).
- **Money:** The US Olympic & Paralympic Committee distributes more than **$100 million a year** in high-performance grants across all sports ([Datawrapper chart of USOPC grants](https://datawrapper.dwcdn.net/2mSYo/4/)). We have not found a recent figure for skateboarding. Most American skaters are mainly paid by their sponsors, not by the federation.
- **What an American Olympian earns:** The USOPC's medal bonuses are **$37,500 for gold, $22,500 for silver and $15,000 for bronze**. On top of that, thanks to a $100 million gift from the financier Ross Stevens, every US Olympian is promised **$200,000**, whether they win or not ([Fortune](https://dc.fortune.com/2026/01/28/billionaire-gift-us-olympics-athletes-100-million-total-200000-apiece)). It is not paid out at once: half is paid later in life, and half is reserved as a benefit for the athlete's family after their death ([Money](https://money.com/olympic-gold-medals-worth-value/?xid=moneyrss)). For most skaters, sponsors are still the main income.
- **Results:** Five medals across Tokyo and Paris, but no American has won Olympic skateboarding gold. LA28 is a home Games in the sport's birthplace, and stars such as Nyjah Huston have [said they want to be there](https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202508/06/WS6892c1dfa3108a99c1905730.html).

### Australia: park champions, state support

- **Money:** The Australian Sports Commission's own grants list shows an agreement with Skate Australia worth **A$4,323,000** (including GST) for **December 2024 to June 2026**, the first 18 months of the LA28 cycle (*official figure*, [ASC grants list 2024](https://www.sportaus.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0006/1181175/Calendar-Year-2024-Grants-List.pdf)). For the six months before that, the Commission allocated A$425,000 for high performance and A$75,000 for talent pathways ([ASC allocation 2024–25](https://sportaus.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0011/1145864/Investment-allocation-2024-25-Website-as-at-07-May-2024.pdf)). Skate Australia also runs other roller sports, but until 2026 it had no participation funding, so the money was for high performance and pathways. The sport is also part of the government's [A$513 million package](https://www.skateaustralia.org.au/post/asc-welcomes-funding-to-prepare-athletes-to-take-on-the-world-s-best) for the next two years, and got participation funding for the first time.
- **Organisation:** Skaters are categorised under the national Australian Institute of Sport system, using its evidence-based framework "What it Takes to Win" ([Skate Australia policy](https://www.skateaustralia.org.au/_files/ugd/f67904_ea8bb644312e42b4a35c19287ad0c01e.pdf)).
- **Results:** Olympic champions **Keegan Palmer** and **Arisa Trew** lead the team into the [LA28 campaign](https://www.olympics.com.au/news/australias-olympic-skateboarding-stars-launch-la28-campaign).

### Great Britain: budget up by half

- **Money:** UK Sport gives skateboarding **£2,550,000** for 2025–2029, up from an award of £1,672,485 for Paris (*official figure*, [UK Sport overview](https://www.uksport.gov.uk/-/media/files/investment/la-2028-investment-funding-awards-overview.ashx)). For Tokyo the figure was £178,165. Skateboarding was one of the sports with an increase of [more than 50%](https://www.cityam.com/?p=2292032).
- **Organisation:** Skateboard GB runs the programme as a "development sport". The federation says the money goes into "systems, expertise and support" for its skaters ([Skateboard GB](https://skateboardgb.org/news/skateboarding-benefits-from-further-progression-funding-from-uk-sport/)).
- **Results:** Sky Brown has won bronze at both Tokyo and Paris.

### France: "aim for gold"

- **Money:** According to the French sports ministry, funding for the French Roller and Skateboard Federation (FFRS) rose by **405%** between 2023 and 2025 (*official figure*, [Ministry of Sport](https://www.sports.gouv.fr/jeux-olympiques-de-milan-cortina-2026-le-sport-francais-au-rendez-vous-de-ses-ambitions-10096)). The federation also covers other roller sports, so not all of this goes to skateboarding.
- **Organisation:** The FFRS has published its [selection philosophy for LA28](https://ffroller-skateboard.fr/wp-content/uploads/PHILOSOPHIE-DE-SELECTION-LA2028.VF_.pdf). It says the goal is "first and foremost gold" and that "participation cannot be an objective". France will not necessarily fill all the places it earns: a skater needs a **top-16** result in 2026 or 2027, or a **top-8** result in 2028.
- **Talent:** Young skaters with potential for **Brisbane 2032** can get reinforced support, but only once their results show that potential.
- **Results:** Aurélien Giraud was street world champion in 2023, and Paris hosted Olympic skateboarding in 2024.

### China: the state system

- **Organisation:** After skateboarding was added to the Olympics, China set up six regional training camps in 2017 and recruited athletes from other sports ([Sixth Tone](https://www.sixthtone.com/news/1008029/team-china-aims-to-conquer-an-unfamiliar-new-sport%3A-skateboarding)).
- **Talent:** The national coach has described a **national reserve team** and a winter camp for talented children aged **9 to 14** ([China Daily](https://chinadailyhk.com/article/583857)). Skateboarding is part of the National Games, and at the first Asian Skateboarding Championships in Meishan in April 2026 China won six golds ([Sichuan news](https://cbgc.scol.com.cn/news/7617138)).
- **Money:** No public figure.

### Sweden: small budget, two Olympians so far

- **Olympic history:** Sweden has had one skater at each Games so far, both in park. **Oskar "Oski" Rozenberg Hallberg** from Malmö finished 17th in Tokyo, and **Hampus Winberg** from Varberg finished 9th in Paris, just outside the final ([Olympedia](https://www.olympedia.org/countries/SWE/sports/SKB.1)).
- **Talent:** Winberg has been in the Swedish Olympic Committee's **Topp och Talang** programme for several years ([TV4](https://www.tv4.se/artikel/71QsDqEjdz4OEupDv0YPlH/17-aring-jagar-os-plats-i-skateboard)). In December 2025, Sveriges Skateboardförbund announced that **Koffe Kroon** had also been selected ([Sveriges Skateboardförbund](https://sverigesskateboardforbund.se/2025/12/19/koffe-kroon-ar-nu-med-i-soks-topp-och-talang/)). Kroon, from Karlshamn, won the Last Chance Open at the big American amateur contest Tampa Am in 2022, ahead of 121 other skaters ([Skatepark of Tampa](https://skateparkoftampa.com/blogs/skaters/koffe-kroon)). In 2026, skateboarder **Vilja Lundmark** received the elite sport scholarship from RF and Svenska Spel, which combines sport with university studies.
- **Money:** Topp och Talang is financed by **sponsors and commercial partners**, not by the state. The Olympic Committee's own survey from 2024 found that in more than half of Sweden's Olympic national teams, athletes pay to compete, on average **close to SEK 100,000 per season** ([SOK report](https://sok.se/download/18.307201031900581e2a53f5a/1719317876818/240612,%20Rapport%20SOK%20%E2%80%93%20Idrottares%20landslagskostnader.pdf), [TV4](https://www.tv4.se/artikel/tt-240612-osekonomi-64cf9a1c/larmet-os-idrottarna-far-betala-for-att-tavla)). The survey covers all Olympic sports, not skateboarding alone. The federation's own travel grant for international contests, Skatestödet, has been closed since May 2025. We have not found a public budget for Sweden's LA28 skateboarding programme.
- **Organisation:** Skateboarding has been a member of the Swedish sports confederation (RF) since [2013](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/se/riksidrottsforbundet/pressreleases/skateboard-ny-idrott-i-riksidrottsfoerbundet-870078). For more on Swedish grants, see our Swedish guide [Bidragsdjungeln](/sv/skateboard/articles/bidrag-och-stod-skateboard-sverige/).

### Other nations to watch

- **Spain:** 15-year-old **Egoitz Bijueska** became park world champion in 2026. We have not found a public budget for Spanish skateboarding.
- **South Korea:** Kang Jun-i [won Korea's first Asian Games skateboarding gold](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/sports/20260927/asian-games-s-korea-wins-gold-in-mens-street-skateboarding-at-asian-games) in 2026, four years after the national team was formed.
- **New Zealand:** The national high-performance agency is [investing in Skateboarding NZ](https://hpsnz.org.nz/?p=10401) to find skaters who can qualify for LA28.

## For every country: the IOC's new athlete grants

In June 2026 the IOC created a fund of more than **$100 million** for **$10,000 grants** that athletes can apply for after competing. It is officially "not prize money". Around 11,000 athletes at LA28 can apply, if they meet conditions such as never having tested positive for doping ([AP via TSN](https://www.tsn.ca/olympics/article/olympic-athletes-to-get-cash-grants-from-new-100m-fund-created-by-ioc/)). For skaters from countries with small budgets, that can be a meaningful amount.


## For the federations: Olympic TV money from 2028

Surfing, skateboarding and sport climbing were added to the Olympics through a fast-track process, and their international federations got **none of the IOC's broadcast revenue** after Tokyo or Paris. The International Surfing Association received only **US$2.7 million** from the IOC to carry it from Tokyo to Paris. Under rules from the federations' association ASOIF, a sport must have been on the programme for three Games in a row to get a full share, so the new sports get it **from LA 2028** ([Front Office Sports](https://frontofficesports.com/article/surfing-thrills-in-2024-olympics-but-faces-funding-hurdles-until-2028/)). After Tokyo, the established sports received between US$13 million and US$39 million each. How much will reach the national federations and their skaters and surfers is not yet known.
## What the money buys

| Country | Public figure we found | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Great Britain | £2.55 million | Skateboarding, 2025–2029 |
| Brazil | R$11.3 million | Skateboarding, 2026 only (lottery money via COB) |
| Japan | 100% / 75% of travel | Top 3 / places 4–8 in each event, plus a national training base |
| France | +405% | The whole roller and skateboard federation, 2023–2025 |
| Australia | A$4.3 million | Skate Australia, December 2024 – June 2026 (government grant, incl. GST) |
| USA, China, Spain, Sweden | Not published per sport | – |

The figures cannot be compared directly, because they cover different periods and different things. But they show the main models: Britain, Australia and Brazil fund a federation programme, Japan pays its ranked skaters directly, and China relies on the state sports system.

## Talent: getting young skaters through qualification

1. **The age limit shapes the programmes.** With a birth-year limit of 2014, federations must decide what to do with very young skaters. Japan puts them in a separate development category, and France supports them only once results show their potential.
2. **Travel is the biggest cost.** Ranking points come from World Skateboarding Tour events around the world. Japan's model, which pays travel based on national ranking, gives its best young skaters the chance to collect points from the start.
3. **Big squads, few places.** Brazil has 34 skaters and Japan up to 32 for a maximum of 12 Olympic places each. The squad is a pool for the ranking battle, not the Olympic team.

## Who has the edge?

*This section is our own assessment, based on the facts above.*

- **Japan** has the most organised system and the most results, and is the favourite to fill most of its 12 places again.
- **Brazil** has depth and money, and stars like Rayssa Leal.
- **The USA** has home advantage and the biggest skate industry, but must turn depth into gold.
- **Australia** remains the park specialist.
- **Europe**, led by France, Britain and Spain, has fewer skaters near the top, and is betting on a few individual medal chances.
